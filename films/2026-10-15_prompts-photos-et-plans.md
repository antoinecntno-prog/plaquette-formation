# Photos et plans réels des deux films de lancement

À utiliser seulement si vous n'avez pas vos propres photos. Huit prompts par film, pour un générateur d'images ; il en faut 6 à 9.

## Format commun

Portrait 4:5, 2160 x 2700 px au minimum, 4000 x 5000 px de préférence. Le film recadre chaque photo en tuile ou en plein cadre : gardez le sujet au centre avec de l'air autour.

Collez ce bloc de style à la fin de chaque prompt :

> Documentary photograph, vertical 4:5 frame. Soft daylight from a side window with a cool neutral white balance. 35 mm lens at f/2.8 with gentle depth of field. Real materials with slight wear. Calm empty space in the upper third of the frame. No readable text and no logos.

## Film dirigeants

1. **Le bureau aux documents.** L'iris s'ouvre sur elle, puis elle devient la tuile centrale du bento.
   > Top-down view of a wooden desk in the office of a small industrial company in eastern France. Printed quotes and technical sheets lie in loose piles next to a pen and a mug of coffee. A laptop sits half open at the edge of the frame. Morning light crosses the desk from the left.

2. **L'équipe autour de la table.**
   > Five colleagues of a small manufacturing company meet around a light wooden table in a plain meeting room. Two of them lean over a printed document while another points at a laptop screen. They range from their twenties to their sixties and wear everyday work clothes. Shot from table height at the end of the table, with faces turned slightly away from the camera.

3. **Les mains sur le clavier.**
   > Close-up of two hands typing on a laptop keyboard on an office desk. The screen glows out of focus with a long document. A printed page with handwritten notes lies beside the laptop. Sharp focus on the fingers.

4. **L'atelier textile.**
   > Wide view of a textile workshop in the Vosges. A worker in an apron marks a pattern with chalk on a long cutting table covered with rolls of technical fabric. Industrial sewing machines line the back wall under high windows. A light haze of dust floats in the daylight.

5. **Le bureau vitré au-dessus de l'atelier.**
   > A small glass-walled office on a mezzanine above a machining workshop. Inside, a manager in a fleece jacket reads a printed contract at a standing desk. Through the glass, CNC machines and pallets of parts sit in soft focus.

6. **La table après la réunion.**
   > Close view of a meeting table just after a meeting. A printed meeting report with handwritten ticks lies next to a closed notebook and two coffee cups. Late morning light.

7. **La fiche technique sur l'établi.**
   > A printed technical drawing on a steel workbench, held flat by a caliper and a machined aluminium part. Fine metal shavings and a pencil lie nearby. Raking light from a workshop window.

8. **La réponse au client.**
   > A woman at an office desk in a small company answers a client email on her laptop while holding a phone to her ear. Shelves with binders and product samples stand behind her. Seen over her shoulder with her face partly hidden.

## Film écoles

1. **La salle de classe.** L'iris s'ouvre sur elle.
   > An empty classroom in a French vocational high school in the morning. Rows of light wooden desks face a whiteboard with the chairs tucked in. Sunlight falls through tall windows onto the floor. Shot from the back of the room at eye level.

2. **Le prof le soir.** Pour cette photo, retirez du bloc de style la phrase sur la lumière du jour.
   > A teacher prepares a lesson at home in the evening. A desk lamp lights a laptop and a printed course with handwritten notes in the margin. A cup of tea and a stack of student papers sit at the edge of the desk. The window behind is dark and the lamp makes a warm pool of light in a cool room.

3. **Les élèves qui révisent.**
   > Two students around twenty revise together at a library table. Handwritten notes and highlighters are spread out between them. One quizzes the other from a sheet. Daylight comes from a large window and the students are seen in profile.

4. **Le couloir.**
   > A bright corridor of a French lycée with students walking past in soft motion blur. Lockers line one side and windows line the other. Late afternoon light.

5. **La classe de BTS au travail.**
   > A small class of BTS students in their early twenties works on laptops and paper. A teacher leans over one desk to look at a student's screen. Natural light falls on the desks and faces turn toward the work.

6. **La scolarité.**
   > The school office of a training centre. A staff member checks a printed schedule of work-study students against a laptop. A wall planner covered with coloured magnets fills the background.

7. **Les cartes de révision.**
   > Close view of a student's desk. A stack of handwritten question cards sits next to a phone showing a blurred quiz screen. Morning light.

8. **Le campus.**
   > Exterior of a modern campus building in a city of the Grand Est region of France, pale sandstone and glass, with students crossing the forecourt. Autumn trees under soft overcast light.

## Les deux plans réels (Pexels)

Le film se contracte dans un écran de ce plan et y pose une image : l'écran doit rester entier dans le cadre et tourné vers la caméra. Gardez-le libre de toute main et de tout reflet fort. Plan fixe de 8 s ou plus de préférence ; la lumière peut bouger. Vertical, ou horizontal en 4K pour recadrer en 4:5.

- **Film dirigeants.** Vue de dessus d'un bureau ou d'une table de réunion en lumière du jour, avec un ordinateur portable ouvert. Recherche Pexels : « laptop desk top view daylight ».
- **Film écoles.** Un pupitre de classe ou un cahier à côté d'un téléphone, avec une lumière naturelle qui bouge. Recherche Pexels : « phone notebook desk sunlight top view ».
